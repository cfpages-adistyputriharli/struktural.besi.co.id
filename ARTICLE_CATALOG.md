# Article Catalog — struktural.besi.co.id

Scope: 102 non-geographic article briefs across 17 structural-steel-profile topics. This catalog is a complete planning inventory, not permission to publish all pages at once.

Domain boundaries: educational pages own identification, evidence, selection inputs, lifecycle tasks, and safety stop conditions. Existing product hubs and `/kontak/` own sales conversion. Structural calculations, substitutions, connection design, welding procedures, lifting, fire engineering, repair, and compliance approval remain with qualified professionals. Other owned domains may cover the same queries independently.

Catalog statistics: 17 topics; 102 briefs; exactly 6 briefs per topic; 14 P0, 34 P1, 40 P2, and 14 P3. No geography-swapped brief is included.

Priority legend: P0 = 14-asset first cluster; P1 = next entity/decision layer; P2 = specialist lifecycle depth; P3 = later reference after evidence capacity exists. Wave 2 starts only after Wave 1 quality, indexation, intent separation, and commercial signals are reviewed.
