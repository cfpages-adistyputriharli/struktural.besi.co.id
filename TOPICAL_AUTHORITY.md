# Topical Authority — struktural.besi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`struktural.besi.co.id` is an Indonesian structural-steel profile knowledge and commercial-support property. Its evidenced catalog covers WF, H-Beam, INP, UNP, CNP, T-Beam, and equal/unequal angle profiles. The editorial role is to help buyers, estimators, designers, fabricators, supervisors, and owners identify profiles correctly, read dimensions and certificates, prepare procurement specifications, compare alternatives, preserve traceability, and recognize when project-specific engineering is mandatory.

The site is not a substitute for structural analysis, connection design, shop drawings, welding procedures, fire engineering, erection planning, statutory approval, or acceptance by qualified professionals. Existing product pages and `/kontak/` own sales conversion; knowledge articles own one educational task each. This plan contains no city/regency swaps. Other Syamsul-owned domains may cover the same queries independently because cannibalization boundaries apply within this domain.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current standards, grades, tolerances, fire requirements, manufacturer data, and regulations from primary sources at publication time; never invent clauses or assume visually similar designations are equivalent.
- Calculators must show formulas, units, input source, assumptions, rounding, tolerance, and explicit limits. Theoretical mass is not proof of delivered mass or structural capacity.
- Structural selection, substitution, connections, welding, lifting, damaged-member disposition, fire performance, repair, and reuse require qualified discipline review.
- Use original/licensed photos and diagrams. Case studies require real orders/projects, permission, traceable material/evidence, constraints, and measured outcomes.
- Claims such as factory-direct, cheapest, SNI, “since 2012,” complete stock, guaranteed grade, or fastest delivery require current proof and precise scope.
- Each article records author, reviewer, evidence date, update trigger, internal links, and commercial disclosure. Safety-critical pages show stop conditions visibly.
